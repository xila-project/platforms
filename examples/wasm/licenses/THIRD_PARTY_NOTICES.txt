Third-Party Font and Icon Notices
==================================

The Xila browser demonstration includes web fonts, browser help-panel SVG icons,
and embedded LVGL glyph subsets rasterized from third-party fonts in
xila-project/core (subsystems/graphics/fonts/).

================================================================================

4. Unscii (unscii-16)
================================================================================

The embedded terminal fonts at 8 and 16 pixels use the base Unscii 2.1
(unscii-16.woff) from Viznut (Ville-Matias Heikkila). Unscii and its source
font Fixedsys Excelsior are public domain (Public Domain / CC0 for Unscii).
The base variant excludes the GNU Unifont fill.

Source and licensing:
